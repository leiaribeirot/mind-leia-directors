# Visual QA Checklist

**Purpose:** Comprehensive checklist for validating Instagram Story quality
**Used by:** Visual QA Specialist (Maya)
**Version:** 1.0.0

---

## 🎯 Overview

This checklist defines the quality standards for Instagram Stories generated by CreativeGenerator. Every story must pass technical validation and achieve minimum scores in craft quality before approval.

---

## ✅ Technical Validation (Critical - Auto-Reject if Fail)

### 1. Dimensions Check ⚠️ CRITICAL

**Requirement:** PNG must be exactly 1080x1920 pixels

**Check:**
- Width = 1080px ✓
- Height = 1920px ✓
- Aspect ratio = 9:16 (0.5625) ✓

**Why Critical:**
- Instagram Stories only accept 9:16 format
- Wrong dimensions = story doesn't fit, gets cropped/letterboxed
- Professional appearance requires exact dimensions

**Auto-Reject:** YES - No exceptions

**Example Issues:**
- ❌ 1080x1080 (square - feed format, not story)
- ❌ 1920x1080 (landscape - wrong orientation)
- ❌ 1080x1350 (4:5 - feed format)

---

### 2. Safe Zones Check ⚠️ CRITICAL

**Requirement:** Critical content must avoid Instagram UI overlay areas

**Top Safe Zone (20% = 384px from top):**
- Instagram UI: profile pic, handle, time
- Critical text/logo: AVOID this area
- Decorative elements: OK

**Bottom Safe Zone (20% = 384px from bottom):**
- Instagram UI: swipe-up, tap actions, reply bar
- Critical text/CTA: AVOID this area
- Decorative elements: OK

**Check:**
- Top 384px clear of critical text ✓
- Bottom 384px clear of critical text ✓
- Important content in middle 60% ✓

**Why Critical:**
- Instagram overlays UI on these areas
- Text here gets covered/unreadable
- Unprofessional appearance

**Auto-Reject:** YES - Critical text in safe zones

**Example Issues:**
- ❌ Headline in top 384px (covered by profile)
- ❌ CTA button in bottom 384px (covered by reply bar)
- ✅ Small tag in top corner (decorative, OK)

---

### 3. Contrast Ratio Check ⚠️ CRITICAL

**Requirement:** Text must be legible (WCAG AA minimum)

**Minimum:** 4.5:1 contrast ratio (WCAG AA)
**Target:** 7:1 contrast ratio (WCAG AAA)

**Check:**
- Light text on dark background ✓
- Dark text on light background ✓
- Sufficient contrast with photo overlay ✓

**Why Critical:**
- Poor contrast = unreadable text
- Accessibility requirement
- Professional quality standard

**Auto-Reject:** YES - Insufficient contrast

**Example Issues:**
- ❌ Light gray text on white background
- ❌ White text on bright photo without scrim
- ✅ White text on black scrim (high contrast)
- ✅ Black text on white box (high contrast)

## 🎨 Craft Quality Validation (Scored 0-100)

### 4. Artisanal Feel (Target: 85+, Reject if < 70)

**Question:** Does it look handmade by a human designer?

**High Score (85-100):**
- ✅ Not template-obvious
- ✅ Strategic line breaks (not just overflow)
- ✅ Custom spacing (not rigid grid)
- ✅ Organic positioning (not perfectly centered)
- ✅ Subtle variations
- ✅ Human touch visible

**Medium Score (70-84):**
- ⚠️ Somewhat template-y but acceptable
- ⚠️ Some rigid elements
- ⚠️ Lacks full craft refinement

**Low Score (0-69):**
- ❌ Obviously template-generated
- ❌ Everything perfectly aligned/symmetric
- ❌ Robotic feel
- ❌ No human touch

**Examples:**

**GOOD (90):**
```
Text has strategic line breaks:
"A grande verdade
é que isso não foi sorte."

(broken for dramatic pause, not overflow)
```

**BAD (60):**
```
Text has automatic line breaks:
"A grande verdade é
que isso não foi sorte."

(broken mid-phrase, robotic)
```

---

### 5. Native Instagram Feel (Target: 90+, Reject if < 75)

**Question:** Does it look like a native Instagram story from a creator?

**High Score (90-100):**
- ✅ Modern Instagram aesthetic
- ✅ Not corporate/ad-like
- ✅ Organic content feel
- ✅ Follows current trends
- ✅ User wouldn't suspect AI
- ✅ Clean, minimal, authentic

**Medium Score (75-89):**
- ⚠️ Mostly native but slight ad feel
- ⚠️ Could be more organic

**Low Score (0-74):**
- ❌ Obvious advertisement
- ❌ Corporate aesthetic
- ❌ Dated design
- ❌ "Generated" look

**Visual Markers of Native Feel:**
- Grayscale or minimal color palette
- System fonts (not decorative)
- One accent color (not rainbow)
- Minimal text (not essay)
- Personal/conversational (not corporate)
- Clean composition (not cluttered)

**Visual Markers of Ad Feel:**
- Multiple bright colors
- Decorative fonts
- Logo-heavy
- Corporate language
- Perfect symmetry
- Overly polished

---

### 6. Typography Hierarchy (Target: 90+, Reject if < 70)

**Question:** Is there a clear typographic hierarchy?

**High Score (90-100):**
- ✅ Clear headline → body → detail levels
- ✅ Maximum 3 levels
- ✅ Obvious size/weight differentiation
- ✅ Easy to scan visually
- ✅ Logical flow

**Medium Score (70-89):**
- ⚠️ Hierarchy exists but not very clear
- ⚠️ Too many levels (4+)
- ⚠️ Some confusion

**Low Score (0-69):**
- ❌ No hierarchy (all same size)
- ❌ Too many levels (5+)
- ❌ Unclear what to read first

**Hierarchy Levels:**
1. **Headline:** Largest, boldest (28-36px, weight 800)
2. **Body:** Medium (16-20px, weight 400)
3. **Detail:** Smallest (12-14px, weight 400)

**Example GOOD:**
```
[HEADLINE - 32px, bold, #30E0C0]
Todo fogo tem uma função

[BODY - 18px, regular, #ffffff]
Ou ele aquece, ou ele transforma.
Qual você vai usar?

[DETAIL - 14px, regular, #b6bcc3]
@leidofogo • leidofogo.com
```

---

### 7. Spacing Quality (Target: 85+, Reject if < 65)

**Question:** Is spacing generous and breathable?

**High Score (85-100):**
- ✅ Line height 1.3-1.6 (readable)
- ✅ Padding 4-6vh (not cramped)
- ✅ Gaps between elements visible
- ✅ Content breathes
- ✅ Not touching edges

**Medium Score (65-84):**
- ⚠️ Slightly tight but acceptable
- ⚠️ Could use more breathing room

**Low Score (0-64):**
- ❌ Cramped (line-height < 1.2)
- ❌ No padding (text touches edges)
- ❌ Elements too close
- ❌ Suffocating composition

**Spacing Rules:**
- Line height: 1.3 (tight), 1.4 (normal), 1.6 (loose)
- Padding: min 4vh, ideal 5vh, max 6vh
- Gap between sections: 2-4vh
- Never touch screen edges with text

---

### 8. Visual Balance (Target: 85+, Reject if < 70)

**Question:** Is the composition visually balanced?

**High Score (85-100):**
- ✅ Text and image don't compete
- ✅ Visual weight distributed
- ✅ Eye not pulled to one side only
- ✅ Harmonious composition

**Medium Score (70-84):**
- ⚠️ Slightly unbalanced but acceptable
- ⚠️ Minor weight issues

**Low Score (0-69):**
- ❌ Very heavy on one side
- ❌ Text covers important image area
- ❌ Awkward composition
- ❌ Obvious imbalance

**Balance Considerations:**
- Photo subject position vs text position
- Visual weight (dark = heavy, light = light)
- Negative space distribution
- Eye flow path

## 📝 Narrative Quality Validation (Pass/Fail)

### 9. Text Density

**Requirement:** 2-5 lines of text per slide

**Check:**
- Count visible lines of text
- Minimum 2 lines (not too empty)
- Maximum 5 lines (not too dense)

**Why Important:**
- < 2 lines: looks empty, wasted space
- > 5 lines: too much text, not readable in 3s
- 2-5 lines: ideal density for story format

**Pass:** 2-5 lines ✓
**Fail:** < 2 or > 5 lines ❌

---

### 10. Readability (3-Second Rule)

**Requirement:** Should be readable in 3 seconds

**Estimation:**
- 150-300 characters = ideal
- < 150 chars = probably readable (but maybe too short)
- 300-400 chars = acceptable
- > 400 chars = probably too much

**Check:**
- Count characters (including spaces)
- Estimate reading time at ~3 chars/sec

**Pass:** ≤ 400 characters ✓
**Fail:** > 400 characters ❌

---

### 11. Emphasis Strategic

**Requirement:** Bold/highlight used purposefully, not excessively

**Check Bold:**
- Bold percentage: 10-20% of total text = good
- < 10% = too little emphasis
- > 20% = too much (loses meaning)

**Check Highlights:**
- 1-2 highlighted phrases per story = good
- 0 highlights = missing opportunity
- 3+ highlights = excessive

**Pass:** Appropriate usage ✓
**Fail:** Over/under emphasis ❌

## 🎯 Scoring Rubric

### Overall Craft Quality Score

**Calculation:**
```
overall_score = (
  artisanal_score +
  native_feel_score +
  typography_score +
  spacing_score +
  balance_score
) / 5
```

**Thresholds:**
- **90-100:** Excellent - Exceeds standards
- **85-89:** Good - Meets target standards ✅ **APPROVE**
- **70-84:** Acceptable - Below target but usable ⚠️ **APPROVE_WITH_NOTES** (if max attempts)
- **0-69:** Poor - Does not meet minimum bar ❌ **REJECT**

### Decision Matrix

| Technical | Craft Score | Attempt | Decision |
|-----------|-------------|---------|----------|
| ✅ Pass | ≥ 85 | Any | ✅ APPROVE |
| ✅ Pass | 70-84 | 1 | ❌ REJECT (retry) |
| ✅ Pass | 70-84 | 2+ | ⚠️ APPROVE_WITH_NOTES |
| ✅ Pass | < 70 | 1 | ❌ REJECT (retry) |
| ✅ Pass | < 70 | 2+ | ⚠️ APPROVE_WITH_NOTES |
| ❌ Fail | Any | Any | ❌ REJECT (critical) |
